# Cost simulator by scale

Move the number of monthly active users and see which plan each service needs, what it
costs per month and per year, and which limits of the current plans are crossed. The
first bottleneck is highlighted.

## How to use it

1. Move the scale control, from 500 to 60,000 monthly active users, or pick one of the
   reference scales: 1,000, 5,000, 10,000 or 50,000.
2. Read the total cost per month and per year, and the bar with the cost breakdown.
3. Check the table: it gives the plan each service needs and its cost.
4. Check the limits crossed and what each one takes: a paid plan, a configuration
   change or a variable cost.

## Where the numbers come from

- The plans, prices and limits are those of
  [Capacity and cost projections](capacity-and-cost-projections.md), with the same
  assumptions and the same date: 2026-10-29. The simulator and that page share one
  model, and an automated test pins its values.
- The fixed costs (development environment, other AWS services, Apple and domain) come
  from [Current costs](costs.md).
- Above 60,000 users the model is no longer valid: a load test is needed.
