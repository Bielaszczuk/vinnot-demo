# Revenue simulator

Try how much Vinnot earns from the ticket commission and from the winery subscription,
and whether that revenue covers the platform costs. Change the commission rate, the
number of events and tickets, the average ticket price, the exchange rate and the number
of wineries on the Pro plan.

## How to use it

1. Pick the commission rate: 3% (implemented), 8% or 12% (planned), or another one.
2. Enter how many paid events there are per month, how many tickets each one sells and
   their average price in pesos.
3. Adjust the exchange rate. The starting value, ARS 1,450 per USD, is an assumption:
   use the day's rate.
4. Move the number of Pro wineries and pick the store fee: 15% with Apple's Small
   Business Program or on Google Play, 30% in the App Store's first year without that
   program.
5. Pick the cost to cover: today's measured cost or the projected cost for 1,000, 5,000,
   10,000 or 50,000 users.

## What it calculates

| Result | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Commission | events × tickets × price × rate, converted to dollars |
| Net subscriptions | Pro wineries × price × (1 − store fee) |
| Margin | total revenue − monthly cost |
| Tickets to cover costs | monthly cost ÷ commission per ticket |
| Wineries to cover costs | monthly cost ÷ net per winery |

## Limits of the calculation

- It is an **estimate**. It excludes taxes (VAT, gross income tax, income tax),
  salaries and marketing.
- The ticket seller pays Mercado Pago's fee, which does not reduce Vinnot's commission.
- The 3% commission is implemented; 8%, 12% and the winery subscription are planned
  ([Monetization and plans](monetization.md)).
- The costs come from the same model as
  [Capacity and cost projections](capacity-and-cost-projections.md) and the
  [cost simulator by scale](cost-simulator.mdx).
