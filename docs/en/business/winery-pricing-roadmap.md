# Winery pricing roadmap

The commercial proposal for offering the Pro plan to a winery: what it costs today, how
it rises as the audience grows and why joining now pays off. It extends the
[winery subscription](monetization.md#winery-subscription), which defines the Free and Pro
plans.

**Status: planned.** The Pro plan does not exist in the product yet. The business idea,
in the owner's words: «hoy como estamos con poca cartera de usuarios el valor de la
suscripción son 20 usd [...] de acá a un año la suscripción va a valer 50 usd, por eso te
conviene congelar el precio ahora, y no se te va a aumentar por 2 años» (today, with a
small user base, the subscription costs USD 20; a year from now it will cost USD 50, so
it pays to lock the price now, and it will not rise for 2 years). The phases, thresholds
and benefits on this page turn that idea into a proposal and can be adjusted. The
audiences come from the base scenario of the
[12-month user projection](user-acquisition-projection.md).

Month 1 is the launch month of the Pro plan for founder wineries, with a target date of
2027-03-01 (status: **planned**, agreed on 2026-11-06); the schedule is in the
[timeline](../gantt/README.md).

## Price phases

| Phase | From | List price | Condition to start | Projected audience at the end of the phase |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Founder wineries | Month 1 | USD 20 a month | 50 winery seats | 6,005 installs and 1,535 active users at month 6 |
| 2. Growth | Month 7 | USD 35 a month | 1,500 monthly active users | 16,301 installs and 2,729 active users at month 12 |
| 3. Scale | Month 13 | USD 50 a month | 2,500 monthly active users | No projection yet: it is recalculated with the first year's data |

The price rises only when both things hold: the month has come and the audience has
reached the threshold. If Vinnot grows more slowly, the price waits. In the base
scenario, phase 2 finds 1,535 active users at month 6 and phase 3 finds 2,729 at month
12, so both start on time. The first threshold matches the moment the operating cost
steps up, when Vinnot leaves the Expo free plan
([Capacity and cost projections](capacity-and-cost-projections.md)).

## The founder winery offer

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Seats | The first 50 wineries that subscribe to the Pro plan |
| Price | USD 20 a month, locked for 24 months from the subscription date |
| After 24 months | Moves to the current list price, with 60 days' notice |
| Ticket commission | Not part of the deal: it follows the [commission roadmap](monetization.md#commission-roadmap) like any organizer |
| If the winery cancels | It loses the founder price; on return it pays the list price |
| Billing | App Store or Google Play subscription, like the rest of the Pro plan ([Monetization and plans](monetization.md#store-economics)) |

The capped seats and the 24-month term limit the risk: the usual discount for a
software product's first customers is 25 to 50% for 6 to 12 months, and lifetime price
locks are discouraged unless the group is capped
([Monetizely](https://www.getmonetizely.com/articles/how-to-choose-the-perfect-pricing-model-for-early-stage-saas-without-scaring-users-away)).
Vinnot's offer is more generous on purpose, because it needs the first wineries so that
there are reports to show.

### What a founder winery saves

| Months | List price | The founder pays | Savings |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | USD 20 | USD 20 | USD 0 |
| 7 to 12 | USD 35 | USD 20 | USD 90 |
| 13 to 24 | USD 50 | USD 20 | USD 360 |
| **Total over 24 months** | | | **USD 450** |

## A founder winery's journey

| Moment | List price | The founder pays | Projected audience | What the winery adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | USD 20 | USD 20 | Launch with QR codes in wine shops and fairs | Profile, catalog loaded with help, events with tickets and the weekly and monthly report |
| Month 7 | USD 35 | USD 20 | 6,005 installs and 1,535 active users at month 6 | More wines pass the 10-person minimum; weekly trend of ratings and reviews |
| Month 13 | USD 50 | USD 20 | 16,301 installs and 2,729 active users at month 12 | Taste profile of its audience and position against wines of its type, grape and region |
| Month 24 | USD 50 | USD 20 | Recalculated with the first year's data | The price lock ends: it saved USD 450 and moves to the list price with 60 days' notice |

## What the winery gets

### From launch, as a founder

- The whole [Free plan](monetization.md#free-plan-and-pro-plan): the winery profile, its
  own wines, events with ticket sales and payments split by Mercado Pago
  ([Payments](../modules/payments/README.md)), the verified winery badge, official
  replies to reviews and basic metrics.
- The team's help to load the catalog of its wines.
- A weekly pulse and a monthly report on the interest, opinion and audience of its
  wines, against peer wines ([Winery reports](winery-reports.md)).
- A founder winery badge and a place on the wine-shop and fair QR codes Vinnot uses to
  gain users.
- A price locked for 24 months.

### Once there are many users

- More wines with data: a report shows a figure only when at least 10 people are behind
  it, to protect privacy. With few users, many wines do not reach that minimum; with more
  users, they do.
- The taste profile of the people who choose its wines: preferred grapes and types.
- Each wine's position against others of the same type, grape or region.
- Where its audience is and how many people reach its events through Vinnot.

## How the price compares

No alternative is identical to the Pro plan, and several figures come from secondary
sources, flagged in each case. Untappd, the Wine Market Council and Wine Intelligence
were surveyed on 2026-11-06.

- **Ads:** at about USD 3.7 per thousand impressions on Meta, USD 20 a month buys about
  5,400 impressions in Argentina
  ([Superads](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/argentina)).
  The Pro plan instead gives data about people who already try the winery's wines.
- **Winery tools:** a booking platform such as CellarPass starts at USD 199 a month,
  according to a review site
  ([Krowdbase](https://www.krowdbase.com/software/cellarpass-guest-management-platform)).
- **Untappd for Business**, the best documented analogue (beer): one-off reports at
  USD 79 to 99 and subscriptions at USD 899 to 1,199 a year per venue, according to
  competitor sources, not official prices
  ([Next Glass](https://nextglass.substack.com/p/introducing-the-untappd-insights);
  [Brewlytics](https://brewlytics.ai/blog/untappd-for-business-pricing)).
- **Wine Market Council:** membership at about USD 400 to 1,000 a year
  ([Wine Industry Advisor](https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2024/02/28/registration-open-wine-market-council-research-conference-2024/)).
- **Wine Intelligence:** market reports at 1,500 to 2,500 pounds each, 2021 prices
  ([Wine Intelligence](https://wineintelligence.com/?p=53616)).

USD 20 a month (USD 240 a year) sits below all of them. At that price the Pro plan
competes as an automatic, interpreted report, not as an analyst service.

## Risks and how they are mitigated

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The audience grows more slowly than the base scenario | The list price does not rise until the active-user threshold is met |
| Few wineries move from the Free plan to Pro on their own | The Free plan's basic metrics show what Pro adds; sales are direct: visits to wineries and fairs with this proposal |
| Reports show little data at first | The 10-person rule is explained from day one; the report shows which wines do not reach it yet |
| 50 wineries locked at USD 20 limit revenue | Even with every seat taken, the Pro plan net of the store fee (USD 17 per winery) adds USD 850 a month, ten times today's operating cost ([Current costs](costs.md)) |
