# Points and seals

Every time you take part in Vinnot you earn points: when you review a wine, when
your reviews get likes, when you comment, when you suggest a new wine or register
for an event. Your points change your **wax seal**, which starts as Bud and can
reach Vinnot. The seal shows next to your name on reviews and on your profile,
so the community can recognise the people who contribute the most.

## In short

1. Take part: every action in the [How to earn points](#how-to-earn-points) table counts.
2. You can earn up to **40 points per day**; wines you suggest that the team approves do not count toward that cap.
3. Your seal follows the points you have today: it goes up when you earn and can go down if points are removed.
4. Tap your seal on your profile to open “Your points”: how to earn, your recent activity and the full seal ladder.

## How to earn points

| Action | Points | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Publish a review | +3 | Once per wine |
| Write a comment of 80 characters or more in your review | +2 | Once per wine |
| Receive a like on one of your reviews | +1 | Up to 5 per day. A like on your own review counts too |
| Comment on someone else's review | +1 | Up to 3 per day |
| Suggest a new wine that the team approves | +8 | No daily limit: it does not count toward the 40 cap |
| Scan a label that exactly matches a catalog wine | +1 | Once per wine, up to 3 per day |
| Add a wine to your cellar by hand | +1 | Once per wine, up to 3 per day |
| Register for an event (confirmed registration) | +4 | Per confirmed registration |
| Complete your profile: photo, first name, last name and phone number | +5 | Once |

- **Giving** likes earns nothing; receiving them does.
- A wine that enters your cellar because you published a review does not earn the cellar point: that wine already earned through the review.
- Only published reviews count. A review under review or rejected earns once it is approved.
- No action earns twice: repeating it on the same content gives no new points.

### The daily cap

You can earn up to 40 points per day, counted in your account's time zone. Wines you
suggest that the team approves earn their 8 points even when you already reached the
cap.

## What removes points

| Situation | What happens to your points |
|---|---|
| You delete a review and write it again | Its points are removed when you delete it and come back when you write it again: you end up where you were, never with extra points |
| The moderation team removes one of your reviews or comments | Only that content's points are removed |
| A review goes back to review, for example after editing a pending or rejected review | It stops counting until it is approved; once approved it earns again, only once |
| Your event registration is cancelled, rejected or expires | That registration's 4 points are removed |
| You permanently delete your account | All your points are deleted |

And what does **not** remove points:

- If a review you commented on is deleted, you keep the points for your comment.
- If someone who liked your review deletes their account, you keep that point.

Every deduction shows in your recent activity as a negative entry.

## The seals

Eleven seals, from the first to the highest. Each one has its own shine, which grows with the level.

Each card shows the seal with its animation, its name and the points you need to reach it.

Seal names follow your language: in Spanish they are Brote, Parra, Racimo, Vendimia,
Lagar, Barrica, Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva, Gran Enófilo and Vinnot. If your device
has reduce motion turned on, the seal stays still with a fixed highlight.

### When you reach a new seal

- The first time you reach a seal, the app and the website celebrate it with the seal on the full screen.
- You also get a push with the name of the seal you reached, if your “Community” notifications are on.
- The celebration and the push come only once per seal: if you drop and climb back, they do not repeat.

### If you already used Vinnot

When points launched, Vinnot counted your earlier activity with the same rules and
the same daily caps, so you start with the seal you earned. Instead of a celebration
you see a welcome card, once, telling you your seal is ready. Label scans from before
the launch do not count, because they were not stored before.

## Where your seal shows

| Place | What you see |
|---|---|
| Top of your profile | Your seal, your points, the seal name and a progress bar to the next one |
| “Your points” (tap the seal on your profile) | How to earn points, your last 20 entries and the full seal ladder |
| A wine's reviews and your own reviews | The seal next to your name |
| Review comments | The seal next to your name |
| User administration | Each account's seal and points, for the team only |

- In lists the seal shines once when it appears and again every 8 to 16 seconds; on your profile it shines all the time.
- The seal does not show on events you organise, blocks, reports or push notifications.
- A locked or deactivated account shows no seal.
- Other people see your seal, not your number of points.

## Rating with glasses

Ratings go from **0.1 to 5.0**, with one decimal, on reviews and in your cellar. If
you want 2.15, it is saved as 2.2.

### How they look

Each glass fills by the fraction of the score: a 2.2 is two full glasses and 0.2 of
the third. The glass, the wine colour and the bottle follow the wine type, as in the
AI profile scene:

| Wine type | Glass | Wine | Bottle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red (or unknown type) | Bordeaux | Red | Dark |
| Fortified | Bordeaux | Ruby | Dark |
| Rosé | Rosé | Rosé | Clear rosé |
| White | Tulip | White | Clear white |
| Orange and dessert | Tulip | Amber | Clear amber |
| Sparkling | Flute, with rising bubbles | Sparkling | Sparkling |

### How to rate

Try it here: pick a wine type and move the bottle over the glasses or slide the bar below.

<PourRating />

1. Before you rate, the bottle stands to the left of the glasses and uncorks itself: a corkscrew pulls the cork (a sparkling cork pops). Underneath there is a bar with a small glass, and the score reads “0.0 / 5”.
2. Drag the bottle towards the glasses, slide the bar or tap a glass: the bottle is lifted over the first glass and the glasses fill together with the bar. Each whole number on the bar sits under its glass.
3. While you raise the score, the bottle tilts, pours a stream of the wine's colour into the glass being filled and empties.
4. While you lower it, the bottle lifts, the glasses empty and the bottle fills again.
5. At 5.0, after the last glass is poured, the bottle goes back to its place, open and empty.
6. The chosen score shows at the end of the bar, for example “3.6 / 5”.

| With | How it adjusts |
|---|---|
| Screen reader | It is a slider: each increment or decrement changes 0.1 |
| Keyboard (web) | Arrows: ±0.1 · Page Up / Page Down: ±0.5 · Home / End: minimum and maximum |

The ratings already in your cellar are kept. Publishing a review copies its rating to
your cellar with its decimal, without rounding.

## FAQ

**Can I lose my seal?**
Yes. The seal follows your current points; if points are removed and you fall below
its threshold, you go back to the previous seal. The celebration does not repeat when
you win it back.

**Why did an action not earn points?**
Check the [How to earn points](#how-to-earn-points) table: you may have reached the
day's 40 cap or that action's daily limit, that wine may have already earned, or the
review may not be approved yet.

**Can I like my own review?**
Yes, and it earns 1 point like any like you receive, within the limit of 5 per day.

**Can I turn off the new seal notification?**
Yes: turn off “Community” in your notification preferences. The celebration inside the
app and the website still shows.

**Is there a ranking?**
No. Points are for your seal; there is no ranking between people and no public
profiles with points.
