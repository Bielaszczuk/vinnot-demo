# Functional requirements

Functional requirements of the module, split by platform. The "Previous ID" column keeps the identifier they had in the per-platform specification. Requirements that were not in that specification show "—".

## Web

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case | Previous ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-WEB-COM-01` | Report content in context. | Review, comment, user, event or route is identified by type/ID; reporting one's own content is rejected, and the action is hidden from the author or the event organizer; a catalog wine or the `CATALOG_QUALITY` reason answers 422 `REPORT_NOT_ALLOWED`. | [`UC-COM-01`](use-cases/uc-com-01-report-content.md) | `RF-WEB-SAFETY-001` |
| `RF-WEB-COM-02` | Select a reason and explain the report. | Spam, harassment, hate, sexual, violence, privacy, misinformation or other; OTHER requires details, up to 2000 characters. | [`UC-COM-01`](use-cases/uc-com-01-report-content.md) | `RF-WEB-SAFETY-002` |
| `RF-WEB-COM-03` | Block and unblock other users. | Contextual confirmation records a personal block; the blocked list allows reversal and is distinct from administrative banning. | [`UC-COM-02`](use-cases/uc-com-02-block-user.md) | `RF-WEB-SAFETY-003` |
| `RF-WEB-COM-04` | View personal cases and overview. | Shows available references, dates, status and counts; private evidence and other cases' internal notes are not exposed. | [`UC-COM-03`](use-cases/uc-com-03-my-community.md) | `RF-WEB-SAFETY-004` |
| `RF-WEB-COM-05` | Follow and unfollow another member. | "Follow" and "Following" toggle without approval through `PUT` and `DELETE /community/follows/{userId}` (204, idempotent); following oneself answers 400 `SELF_FOLLOW` and a missing, inactive or blocked account 404; on failure the button returns to its state and the client says so. | [`UC-COM-05`](use-cases/uc-com-05-follow-member.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-06` | Award one seal point per follower. | A new follow by someone with a verified email and at least one approved review awards 1 `FOLLOWER_GAINED` point, up to 10 a day within the global cap of 40; unfollowing or blocking takes it back; repeating the follow awards nothing more. | [`UC-COM-05`](use-cases/uc-com-05-follow-member.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-07` | View a member's public profile. | `/users/{id}` shows photo, name, @username, bio, seal, the "Followers", "Following", "Reviews" and "Wines in cellar" counters, "Follows you", the affinity in % (when both rated five wines) and the approved reviews; the own profile shows "Edit profile" instead of "Follow"; a missing, inactive or blocked account is not shown. | [`UC-COM-06`](use-cases/uc-com-06-view-public-profile.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-08` | Show the cellar according to its visibility. | `PUBLIC` is visible to every member, `FOLLOWERS` only to followers and `PRIVATE` to nobody; the owner always sees it; when hidden, the counter shows "—" and the section a locked state (403 `CELLAR_NOT_VISIBLE`); private notes and purchase prices are never shown. | [`UC-COM-06`](use-cases/uc-com-06-view-public-profile.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-09` | Open the profile of a review's author. | The name and photo of the author of a wine review or a feed review, a followers or following row and a suggestion open their public profile. | [`UC-COM-06`](use-cases/uc-com-06-view-public-profile.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-10` | View followers and following. | The counters open the followers or following list, from the newest follow to the oldest and without blocked accounts, with photo, name, seal, @username and "Follow" on every row except the own one. | [`UC-COM-07`](use-cases/uc-com-07-view-follow-lists.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-11` | See reviews from people one follows. | At the end of Home, "From people you follow" shows the three newest approved reviews of followed people and "See all" opens `/feed`, 10 per page and newest first; without reviews, it invites the person to follow others. | [`UC-COM-08`](use-cases/uc-com-08-following-feed-and-suggestions.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-12` | Suggest people to follow. | "People to follow" shows up to six active members with at least five rated wines, not followed, without a block and other than oneself, ranked by affinity and then by reviews, with their affinity, reviews and "Follow"; without suggestions, it shows a notice. | [`UC-COM-08`](use-cases/uc-com-08-following-feed-and-suggestions.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-13` | Edit the bio and the cellar visibility. | The "Public profile" card at `/profile` saves through `PATCH /users/me` a bio of up to 160 characters with a counter (empty clears it; longer, 400 `BIO_TOO_LONG`) and "Who can see your cellar": "Public" (default), "Followers only" or "Private". | [`UC-COM-09`](use-cases/uc-com-09-edit-public-profile.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-14` | View the illustrated taste profile. | The card shows the wheel, "Structure" with acidity, body, tannin and sweetness on illustrated scales (low, medium or high; dry, off-dry or sweet) and "Preferred aromas" with the families and their percentage; a value that comes from the quiz is marked "Based on your taste quiz" or "Based on the taste quiz". | [`UC-COM-10`](use-cases/uc-com-10-view-taste-profile.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-15` | Explore the wheel by grapes, styles or regions. | The "Grapes" (default), "Styles" and "Regions" selector shows one kind at a time; tapping a slice or its legend row highlights it, fades the rest and shows the name, the score out of 5 and the rated wines in the centre; tapping it again or switching views clears the highlight. | [`UC-COM-11`](use-cases/uc-com-11-explore-taste-wheel.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-16` | Paint the wheel as watercolor with the length following the score. | Equal-width slices painted as watercolor; within the view, the best score reaches the rim and the lowest fills 65% of the ring (equal scores: all full); grapes take their tone's colour, styles their wine type's colour and regions distinct hues; with reduced motion there is no animation. | [`UC-COM-11`](use-cases/uc-com-11-explore-taste-wheel.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-17` | Lock the wheel until five rated wines. | With fewer than five rated wines the wheel is not shown: a locked state explains why, or a compact notice takes its place when there are already quiz traits or adjusted values. | [`UC-COM-10`](use-cases/uc-com-10-view-taste-profile.md), [`UC-COM-11`](use-cases/uc-com-11-explore-taste-wheel.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-18` | Answer the taste quiz. | Five illustrated questions one at a time ("Question n of 5"), with "Back" and "Skip" on every step; at the end, `PUT /users/me/taste-quiz` stores the five answers and the own profile opens; retaking it replaces the answers; the member never sees how they translate into traits. | [`UC-COM-12`](use-cases/uc-com-12-take-taste-quiz.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-19` | Offer the quiz once and allow skipping it. | After sign-in it is offered once per browser session to members with a pending quiz and fewer than five rated wines; "Skip" records `SKIPPED` and it is not offered on its own again, without erasing a completed quiz; the own profile offers "Take the taste quiz" or "Retake the quiz". | [`UC-COM-12`](use-cases/uc-com-12-take-taste-quiz.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-20` | Animate the creation of the taste profile. | While saving, "Defining your taste profile" shows four scenes of its own ("Gathering your picks", "Filling your glass", "Blending your flavors" and "Shaping your taste profile") of about 3 seconds each and at least 13.4 seconds in all; a failure keeps the answers and offers "Try again" or "Not now". | [`UC-COM-12`](use-cases/uc-com-12-take-taste-quiz.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-COM-21` | Adjust one's own palate. | On the own profile, "Adjust" turns the four bars into 0-100 sliders used with the pointer or the keyboard; "Save" sends only the changed traits, "Cancel" discards and "Reset to calculated" clears the hand-set values; an adjusted trait shows "Adjusted". | [`UC-COM-13`](use-cases/uc-com-13-adjust-palate.md) | — |
