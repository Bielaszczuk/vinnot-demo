# Functional requirements

Functional requirements of the module, split by platform.

## Web

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-WEB-MKT-01` | Plan the week automatically and on demand. | On Mondays at 09:00 Argentina time, and when "Planificar ahora" is pressed, rotating posts are created for the next 14 days on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday at 12:30; the response reports `created`, `from` and `to`; repeating creates nothing. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-02` | Post wine days on their day. | A wine day inside the window is posted that day at 09:30; on a slot day it takes that day's slot (no rotating post that day); of two wine days on one date the lower priority number wins and the other is dropped. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-03` | Write the copy with AI and verify it. | Each post gets hook, slides, caption, hashtags, alt and CTA within the limits, with the legal line appended by the system; a failed check regenerates once and flags `FLAGGED`; a provider failure leaves the draft with its error and retries. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-04` | Notify when a batch is ready for review. | Every administrator gets an email with the count and the window and a button to the queue, plus a push that opens the app; a run with no new posts does not notify. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-05` | See the queue by status. | The page lists posts by status (pending by default, tabs for every status) sorted by date, with pillar and wine-day chips. | [`UC-MKT-02`](use-cases/uc-mkt-02-approve-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-06` | Preview a post. | The rendered slides show (or text cards while the post has no slides yet), the caption with the legal line, hashtags, alt text, CTA and the generation info (model, mock, fact check with its issues). | [`UC-MKT-02`](use-cases/uc-mkt-02-approve-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-07` | Approve, reject, edit and regenerate. | Approving records who and when; rejecting takes an optional reason; editing validates the limits and returns to pending; regenerating asks for both languages again, also on an approved post, which then loses its approval and goes back to pending; an invalid action answers 409 and invalid copy 400. | [`UC-MKT-02`](use-cases/uc-mkt-02-approve-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-08` | Consult the wine-day calendar. | The API returns a year's days with their resolved date, sorted; the page shows those of the current and next month. | [`UC-MKT-03`](use-cases/uc-mkt-03-wine-day-calendar.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-09` | Paint the hero illustration with AI in the Vinnot style. | With `ai.images.enabled` set to `true`, every post gets an OpenAI illustration (`/v1/images/edits`) with the app illustrations as references and the watercolor recipe; the image is requested with a transparent background and checked (alpha channel, transparent corners and at least 90% of the border) before use; the subject comes from the copy's `illustration` field, or from the wine day or the pillar; each image reserves `ai.images.max-cost-usd` against the AI cap. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-10` | Use the pillar vignette when there is no AI illustration. | When AI images are off, in mock mode, over budget, when the image is not transparent, when the request fails after its retries or when the paid image cannot be stored, the cover uses one of the nine pillar vignettes and the media keeps the reason (`AI_IMAGE_UNAVAILABLE`, `AI_IMAGE_REJECTED`, `AI_IMAGE_FAILED`, `HERO_STORE_FAILED`, `INVALID_ILLUSTRATION`); a post never ends without slides because of the illustration. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-11` | Draw the slides for Instagram and TikTok. | Every post has JPEG slides of 1080x1350 (Instagram) and 1080x1920 (TikTok): cover with illustration, hook and mark; one card per copy slide; closing card with CTA and legal line; paper background, vine ornaments and the bundled Cormorant Garamond and Poppins fonts; on TikTok the text stays in the central safe band; the text wraps and shrinks until it fits and, when it cannot, fails with `TEXT_DOES_NOT_FIT` instead of being cut. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-12` | Store the images with a public URL. | The illustration and the slides are stored with the configured storage (local, S3 or R2) under `social/posts/<id>/…` with a hash of the content, one write per key and up to 5 MB; local and S3 serve them at `GET /api/uploads/social/{key}` and R2 at `https://images.vinnot.com/uploads/{environment}/social/{key}`, cached as immutable; R2 keeps to its monthly social byte allowance; replaced images are deleted only when nothing uses them anymore. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-13` | Render in the background without showing stale slides. | Generating, regenerating or editing what is drawn queues a durable `social-media-render` job; the media state (`PENDING`, `READY`, `FAILED` with error, source `AI` or `FALLBACK`) is stored in `social_posts.media`; editing only the caption, hashtags or alt text does not render again; slides of older copy are never returned; a transient error is retried up to five attempts (`RENDER_ATTEMPTS_EXHAUSTED`); a sweep every 30 minutes queues the upcoming posts still waiting. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-14` | See and download the slides on the approval page. | The page shows the slides per format (Instagram or TikTok), the "AI illustration" or "Pillar vignette" badge with the reason, the "Rendering the slides…" note while pending (polling every 4 seconds), the translated error when they failed and one download link per slide. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-15` | Request a new illustration. | "New illustration" sends `POST /admin/social-posts/{id}/illustration`, which answers 202, bumps the illustration version and renders again without changing the post status; a post without copy or already published answers 409 and the web disables the button. | [`UC-MKT-05`](use-cases/uc-mkt-05-new-illustration.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-16` | Write every post in Spanish and English. | One AI call writes the post in neutral Spanish ("tú", no voseo or regional slang) and in English with the same facts; the fact check covers both; the post waits for approval only when both copies are valid; each language has its own legal line ("Beber con moderación. Prohibida su venta a menores de 18 años." / "Drink responsibly. Not for sale to anyone under 18.") and hashtags with `#vinnot`; the API returns `copies.es` and `copies.en` (null while missing) and `PUT /{id}` edits the language named in `language` (`es` or `en`, otherwise 400 `INVALID_POST_LANGUAGE`). | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md), [`UC-MKT-02`](use-cases/uc-mkt-02-approve-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-17` | Write refined, technical, global content. | The voice is a sommelier's for curious wine lovers worldwide, with technical terms explained in a few words; every post carries concrete, verifiable facts (origin and history, regions, body, acidity, tannin, aromatics, winemaking, ageing potential) and a wine-day post explains why the day exists with at least two facts; no Argentina framing unless the topic is Argentine and no local time; pairings only by food category explained by structure (fat, acidity, tannin, salt), never street or fast food or a single home dish; the pillar briefs (Spanish and English, V81) follow the same rules. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-18` | Render one slide set per language. | The hero is shared; slides render per language and format under `posts/{id}/{es|en}/{format}-NN-{hash}.jpg`; each language tracks its own copy hash, so editing one language renders only that one; the media is `READY` when every language with copy is rendered; the API returns `media.slides.es` and `media.slides.en` (`{instagram[], tiktok[], renderedFor}` or null). Posts stored before V81 keep their copy and slides as Spanish and get English by regenerating, also when approved. | [`UC-MKT-04`](use-cases/uc-mkt-04-render-post-media.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-19` | Publish by itself at the scheduled time. | Every 5 minutes, an `APPROVED` post whose slot has arrived (within the last 2 × `media-grace`, 12 h by default) and whose Spanish slides are ready in both formats moves to `SCHEDULED` and gets one `QUEUED` publication per enabled network, each queued as a `social-publish` durable effect due at that network's time (`RF-WEB-MKT-33`); each network publishes a carousel of the Spanish slides (the Instagram ones for Instagram, Facebook and Threads; the TikTok ones for TikTok) with the Spanish copy; a post older than the window is never published late. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-20` | Fit the copy to each network. | Instagram and Facebook: caption with the legal line and hashtags, at most 2,200 characters and 30 hashtags, alt text on the Instagram slides; Threads: hook, caption shortened with "…" and legal line within 500 characters and one topic; TikTok: title (hook) up to 90 and description with hashtags up to 4,000; the legal line is never cut. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-21` | Track each network and settle the post. | Each per-network publication keeps status (`QUEUED`, `PUBLISHING`, `PUBLISHED`, `DRAFT_SENT`, `FAILED`), network id, link, attempts, error and timestamps; an atomic claim prevents publishing twice; a transient error is retried up to 5 attempts; the post becomes `PUBLISHED` when every network succeeded (a TikTok draft counts) or `FAILED` with the first error; slides not ready 6 h after the slot: `MEDIA_NOT_READY`; a non-https URL: `MEDIA_NOT_PUBLIC`; publishing or the network off: `PUBLISHING_OFF`; a `SCHEDULED` or `PUBLISHED` post is not edited, regenerated or rejected and gets no new illustration. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-22` | Keep TikTok as a draft until the audit. | With `MARKETING_PUBLISHING_TIKTOK_MODE=MEDIA_UPLOAD` (the default) TikTok gets the carousel as a draft in the account's inbox and the publication becomes `DRAFT_SENT`; once the creator posts the draft from the TikTok app, a sweep every 15 minutes marks it `PUBLISHED` with "View on TikTok" when TikTok gives the public post id, and stops checking drafts older than 14 days (#516); with `DIRECT_POST` it is published directly with the most open privacy the account offers. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-23` | Tell the administrators what publishing needs. | Every administrator gets an email and a push: `SOCIAL_POST_FAILED` once per failed run (with the network and the reason, or without a network when the slides were never ready), `SOCIAL_TIKTOK_DRAFT_WAITING` once per post and `SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_RECONNECT` once per network and day when an account stops working. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md), [`UC-MKT-10`](use-cases/uc-mkt-10-connect-network-account.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-24` | See each network and retry the one that failed. | The detail shows per network the status, "View on <network>" with the link, the attempts, the error, the date and the latest metrics, plus the post's publishing start, end and error; "Retry" on a `FAILED` network sends `POST /admin/social-posts/{id}/publications/{network}/retry`, which answers 202, resets the attempts and queues it; a network that is not `FAILED` answers 409 `INVALID_PUBLICATION_TRANSITION`, slides that are not ready 409 `INVALID_POST_TRANSITION` and publishing or the network off 409 `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_OFF`. | [`UC-MKT-07`](use-cases/uc-mkt-07-retry-publication.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-25` | Measure the publications every day. | With `MARKETING_METRICS_ENABLED`, every day at 06:00 (planner zone) views, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares and new follows, when the network gives them, are read for every publication of the last 30 days and one snapshot per publication and day is stored; TikTok gives no metrics; the page shows the latest snapshot per network. | [`UC-MKT-08`](use-cases/uc-mkt-08-collect-metrics-weight-pillars.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-26` | Weight the pillars by their performance. | The planner picks each rotating pillar by fewest uses divided by (weight × factor); the factor is the pillar's interactions per view over the last 90 days against the overall ones, between 0.5 and 2.0, neutral until 3 measured posts; wine days, `APP_FEATURE` once every four and never repeating the previous pillar stay; the pillars panel shows measured posts, views, interactions and factor. | [`UC-MKT-08`](use-cases/uc-mkt-08-collect-metrics-weight-pillars.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-27` | Auto-approve by pillar. | "Auto-approve" (`PUT /admin/social-posts/pillars/{code}/auto-approve`, off by default) makes a pending post of the pillar, with ready slides, a `PASSED` fact check and a future slot 24 h away or less, `APPROVED` without a reviewer with reason `AUTO_APPROVED`; a post a person changed meanwhile is never overwritten; the page shows "Approved automatically". | [`UC-MKT-09`](use-cases/uc-mkt-09-auto-approve-by-pillar.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-28` | Switch a pillar on or off. | "Active" (`PUT /admin/social-posts/pillars/{code}/active`) takes the pillar out of the planner or puts it back; switching `WINE_DAY` off stops planning wine days; switching off the last active rotating pillar answers 409 `LAST_ROTATING_PILLAR`; an unknown code answers 404. | [`UC-MKT-09`](use-cases/uc-mkt-09-auto-approve-by-pillar.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-29` | Connect Threads and TikTok from the panel. | "Connect" asks for `GET /admin/social-accounts/{network}/connect`, which returns the authorization URL with a single-use 10-minute `state`; the redirect to `/api/public/social-accounts/{network}/callback` stores the tokens encrypted with AES-256-GCM and redirects to `/admin/social-posts?account=<network>&result=connected` or `result=error`; Instagram and Facebook show as configured by the server (422 `SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_NOT_OAUTH` on connect, 409 `SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_CONFIGURED_BY_SECRETS` on disconnect); without the app configured 422 `SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_NOT_CONFIGURED`; without the key 422 `SOCIAL_ACCOUNT_ENCRYPTION_KEY_MISSING`; no token leaves the server. | [`UC-MKT-10`](use-cases/uc-mkt-10-connect-network-account.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-30` | Refresh tokens automatically and ask for reconnection. | Threads is refreshed in its last 10 days and TikTok in its last half hour; a refusal, an expired credential or an unreadable token (`TOKEN_UNREADABLE`) leave the account `REFRESH_FAILED` and notify once; the panel shows the status, the account, the expiry and the last error, and "Reconnect". | [`UC-MKT-10`](use-cases/uc-mkt-10-connect-network-account.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-31` | Switch everything on by configuration. | Publishing (`MARKETING_PUBLISHING_ENABLED`), each network (`MARKETING_PUBLISHING_<NETWORK>_ENABLED`), metrics (`MARKETING_METRICS_ENABLED`) and auto-approval (`MARKETING_AUTO_APPROVE_ENABLED`) are off by default; TikTok starts in `MEDIA_UPLOAD`. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-32` | One post per date and a weekly maximum. | At most one post per Argentina date; a wine day off the slot days is an extra post: priority 1 always, priority 2 and 3 only while the week (Monday to Sunday, existing and planned posts) has fewer than `MARKETING_PLANNER_MAX_POSTS_PER_WEEK` (4) posts; existing posts count by date whatever their time, so changing the times or moving a post never adds a second post on a date or a post to its week when planning again. | [`UC-MKT-01`](use-cases/uc-mkt-01-plan-week.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-33` | Publish each network at its time. | `MARKETING_PUBLISHING_<NETWORK>_NOT_BEFORE` (local time in the planner zone, TikTok `19:30` by default, empty for the rest) delays that network's publication until that time of the post's day when it is later than the post; the post stays `SCHEDULED` until every network is done; the response's `networkSchedule` lists `{network, publishAt}` for each network that publishes. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-34` | Change a post's date and time. | "Change date and time" sends `PUT /admin/social-posts/{id}/schedule` with `{"scheduledFor": "<ISO-8601 instant>"}` and answers 200 with the post, its status unchanged; allowed in `DRAFT`, `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED` and `REJECTED` (otherwise 409 `INVALID_POST_TRANSITION`); a time not in the future answers 400 `SOCIAL_POST_SCHEDULE_NOT_FUTURE` and another post on that Argentina date 409 `SOCIAL_POST_DATE_TAKEN`; the response's `canReschedule` says whether it applies; the client warns that a wine-day post's copy mentions its day. | [`UC-MKT-11`](use-cases/uc-mkt-11-reschedule-post.md) |
| `RF-WEB-MKT-35` | Publish a post now. | "Publish now" sends `POST /admin/social-posts/{id}/publish-now` and answers 202 with the post `SCHEDULED` at the current time, queued on every enabled network without their not-before wait; it needs `APPROVED` (409 `INVALID_POST_TRANSITION`), ready Spanish slides (409 `SOCIAL_POST_MEDIA_NOT_READY`) and publishing on with a network enabled (409 `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_OFF`); the response's `canPublishNow` says whether it applies. | [`UC-MKT-12`](use-cases/uc-mkt-12-publish-post-now.md) |

## APK

The app's approval queue is described in the [mobile requirements](../../frontend-mobile/use-cases/functional-requirements.md). App requirements for publishing to the networks:

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-APK-MKT-01` | See each network, its metrics and retry in the app. | The post sheet shows per network the status, the link, the attempts, the error and the latest metrics; "Retry" on a `FAILED` network calls the same endpoint as the web and reports the 409s. | [`UC-MKT-06`](use-cases/uc-mkt-06-publish-post.md), [`UC-MKT-07`](use-cases/uc-mkt-07-retry-publication.md), [`UC-MKT-08`](use-cases/uc-mkt-08-collect-metrics-weight-pillars.md) |
| `RF-APK-MKT-02` | Change the pillar switches in the app. | The screen lists the pillars with their performance and the "Active" and "Auto-approve" switches, with the same endpoints and errors as the web. | [`UC-MKT-08`](use-cases/uc-mkt-08-collect-metrics-weight-pillars.md), [`UC-MKT-09`](use-cases/uc-mkt-09-auto-approve-by-pillar.md) |
| `RF-APK-MKT-03` | See the network connections in the app. | The screen shows each network with its status, account and expiry read-only; connecting and reconnecting happen on the web. | [`UC-MKT-10`](use-cases/uc-mkt-10-connect-network-account.md) |
| `RF-APK-MKT-04` | Change the date and time or publish now in the app. | The post sheet offers "Change date and time" (date and time in Argentina time, with the wine-day warning) and "Publish now" (with confirmation) when `canReschedule` and `canPublishNow` allow them, shows the time of each network and reports the same errors as the web. | [`UC-MKT-11`](use-cases/uc-mkt-11-reschedule-post.md), [`UC-MKT-12`](use-cases/uc-mkt-12-publish-post-now.md) |
