# Functional requirements

Functional requirements of the module, split by platform. The "Previous ID" column keeps the identifier they had in the per-platform specification.

## Web

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case | Previous ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-WEB-NOT-01` | Choose which notifications to receive by email and push. | Shows nine categories with email and push; account, security, payments and tickets are mandatory and cannot be turned off; each change is saved with `PUT /users/me/notification-preferences`. | [`UC-NOT-01`](use-cases/uc-not-01-manage-preferences.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-02` | Grant or withdraw marketing consent. | Recommendations and news start on and consent counts as granted; withdrawing it or turning the category off stops them; when and where it was decided is saved. | [`UC-NOT-01`](use-cases/uc-not-01-manage-preferences.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-03` | Unsubscribe from an email with its link. | Promotional emails carry a link to `/unsubscribe` and the mail client's one-click unsubscribe; the page only applies it after confirming, turns off that category's email and leaves consent unchanged; an invalid link shows an error. | [`UC-NOT-02`](use-cases/uc-not-02-unsubscribe.md) | — |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-04` | Receive verification and recovery links. | Registration/resending and recovery generate messages linking to the corresponding flow; emails do not contain passwords. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-001` |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-05` | Confirm a new email and notify the old one. | A link is sent to the proposed email; confirmation notifies the previous email without changing it before token validation. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-002` |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-06` | Notify a submitted-wine decision. | The transactional flow communicates a status change and available notes; this is distinct from a phone push notification. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-003` |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-07` | Retain branded structure and plain-text alternative. | HTML messages use a banner, logo, image, content/action and web/contact/privacy footer; text versions retain links. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-004` |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-08` | Send each email in the account language. | The language saved on the account is used, which the web and the app update when the interface language changes; without a saved language, Spanish. Announcements keep the language they were requested in. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-005` |
| `RF-WEB-NOT-09` | Confirm the request without promising instant delivery. | Account and support requests are confirmed once the email is queued; sending happens later and is retried if the provider fails, without guaranteeing immediate receipt. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-WEB-MAIL-006` |

## APK

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case | Previous ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-APK-NOT-01` | Choose which notifications to receive by email and push. | The preferences sheet shows nine categories with email and push, “Reminders” included and on by default; account, security, payments and tickets are mandatory; turning on a push asks for the system permission when it was never asked, and without permission the sheet offers “Allow notifications” or “Open Settings”. | [`UC-NOT-01`](use-cases/uc-not-01-manage-preferences.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-02` | Grant or withdraw marketing consent. | Recommendations and news start on and consent counts as granted; withdrawing it or turning the category off stops them; when and where it was decided is saved. | [`UC-NOT-01`](use-cases/uc-not-01-manage-preferences.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-03` | Ask for notification permission. | The first launch shows, signed in or not, a Vinnot sheet that explains notifications before the system prompt; “Turn on” opens the prompt and “Not now” shows nothing else in that launch. If the person declines, the phone is not registered and is removed from the account. While the permission is not granted and someone is signed in, every launch from scratch (never a return from the background) shows a dismissible reminder that requests the permission or opens Settings. The Android channels are created before the permission is requested. | [`UC-NOT-03`](use-cases/uc-not-03-allow-push.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-04` | Register the device for push. | With permission granted and a session, the Expo token is sent with `PUT /users/me/push-devices` with platform, language and, on Android, `channels: true` once the per-category channels exist; it is sent again on every launch from scratch, on sign-in, language change, token rotation and return to the foreground without a registration; without permission the phone's last token is removed from the account; on sign-out the token is sent to unregister it. | [`UC-NOT-04`](use-cases/uc-not-04-register-device.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-05` | Open a notification's section. | Tapping the notification, with the app open or from a cold start, reports the open with `POST /users/me/push-opens` when the push carries `ref` and `type`, and opens its route, once navigation is ready, only if it passes link validation and is an allowed section (tickets, events, wines, routes, wineries, partner events); anything else is ignored. Without a session it does not navigate: the section opens after sign-in. | [`UC-NOT-05`](use-cases/uc-not-05-receive-push.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-06` | Respect quiet hours and push limits. | Pushes that can wait are postponed between 22:00 and 08:00 in the account's time zone; non-mandatory categories have a cap of 3 a day, nearby events 1 a day and recommendations 1 every 7 days. Personalised suggestions do not use that cap: they have their own budget of 2 every 7 days, 1 a day and 48 hours between two, and never go out before 09:00 ([`UC-NOT-07`](use-cases/uc-not-07-receive-suggestions.md)). | [`UC-NOT-05`](use-cases/uc-not-05-receive-push.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-07` | Receive verification and recovery links. | Registration/resending and recovery generate messages linking to the corresponding flow; emails do not contain passwords. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-001` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-08` | Confirm a new email and notify the old one. | A link is sent to the proposed email; confirmation notifies the previous email without changing it before token validation. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-002` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-09` | Notify a submitted-wine decision. | The transactional flow communicates a status change and available notes; this is distinct from a phone push notification. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-003` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-10` | Retain branded structure and plain-text alternative. | HTML messages use a banner, logo, image, content/action and web/contact/privacy footer; text versions retain links. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-004` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-11` | Send each email in the account language. | The language saved on the account is used, which the web and the app update when the interface language changes; without a saved language, Spanish. Announcements keep the language they were requested in. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-005` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-12` | Confirm the request without promising instant delivery. | Account and support requests are confirmed once the email is queued; sending happens later and is retried if the provider fails, without guaranteeing immediate receipt. | [`UC-NOT-06`](use-cases/uc-not-06-receive-email.md) | `RF-APK-MAIL-006` |
| `RF-APK-NOT-13` | Report activity to personalise pushes. | While signed in, the app reports each open (on a cold start and on returning to the foreground, at most one every 30 minutes) with `POST /users/me/activity/app-open` and each wine, route, event or winery detail opened with `POST /users/me/activity/views`, without waiting for the answer and without blocking the screen if it fails; without a session it reports nothing. | [`UC-NOT-07`](use-cases/uc-not-07-receive-suggestions.md) | — |
| `RF-APK-NOT-14` | Receive personalised suggestions within a budget. | At most one suggestion a day, 2 every 7 days and 48 hours between two, at the account's usual app-open hour or at 19:30 on weekdays and 12:30 at weekends, never between 22:00 and 09:00; it respects each category's preferences, drops to 1 a week after 3 unopened and pauses after 28 days without opening any; after 14 days without opening the app only come-back pushes arrive; 5% of accounts stay as a holdout group without suggestions. | [`UC-NOT-07`](use-cases/uc-not-07-receive-suggestions.md) | — |
