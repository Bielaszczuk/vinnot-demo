# Functional requirements

Functional requirements of the module, split by platform. The "Previous ID" column keeps the identifier they had in the per-platform specification.

## Web

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion | Use case | Previous ID |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RF-WEB-USR-01` | Register an account with initial USER role. | Requests username, email, first/last name, password/confirmation and terms acceptance; duplicates or invalid data do not complete registration. | [`UC-USR-01`](use-cases/uc-usr-01-sign-up.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-001` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-02` | Validate registration username and password. | Username is normalized to lowercase, 3–40 characters using letters/digits/dot/hyphen/underscore; password needs at least eight characters, uppercase, lowercase and a digit. | [`UC-USR-01`](use-cases/uc-usr-01-sign-up.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-002` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-03` | Prevent simultaneous authentication operations. | While login, registration or a provider is busy another submission is blocked; cancellation is not presented as success. | [`UC-USR-01`](use-cases/uc-usr-01-sign-up.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-012` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-04` | Verify email and request another message. | The link processes its token; success differs from missing/invalid/expired tokens and the registration flow offers another verification message. | [`UC-USR-02`](use-cases/uc-usr-02-verify-email.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-003` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-05` | Sign in and report rejection. | Only a valid response establishes a session; invalid credentials, unverified email, locked or inactive accounts do not grant access. | [`UC-USR-03`](use-cases/uc-usr-03-sign-in.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-004` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-06` | Use email and password. | The web form validates email format; usernameOrEmail support in the API does not make this field a username login. | [`UC-USR-03`](use-cases/uc-usr-03-sign-in.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTHX-001` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-07` | Sign in using an enabled social provider. | Backend validates the identity; cancellation, missing SDK or invalid configuration do not create a fictitious session. | [`UC-USR-04`](use-cases/uc-usr-04-social-sign-in.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-005` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-08` | Use Google or Apple on the web. | Google prepares its official control before the first click; Apple uses its configured popup. Facebook is displayed only when enabled outside production. | [`UC-USR-04`](use-cases/uc-usr-04-social-sign-in.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTHX-002` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-09` | Complete onboarding for a new social account. | Requests an available username before continuing; this grants no administrative access and does not require inventing a social password. | [`UC-USR-05`](use-cases/uc-usr-05-complete-profile.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-006` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-10` | Restore the session when opening the client. | Identity is resolved before navigation is decided; an expired token uses the session renewal flow without displaying another account's data. | [`UC-USR-06`](use-cases/uc-usr-06-restore-session.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-007` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-11` | Coordinate sessions across tabs. | Coordinated logout and renewal prevent restoring a closed session; blocked storage reduces coordination to local memory. | [`UC-USR-06`](use-cases/uc-usr-06-restore-session.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTHX-003` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-12` | Resume a protected destination after login. | A valid internal destination is remembered; terms and onboarding must complete before opening the requested resource. | [`UC-USR-07`](use-cases/uc-usr-07-resume-protected-destination.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-008` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-13` | Sign out and remove personal client data. | The session is revoked and identity/private cache cleared; failed logout is not reported as confirmed remote revocation. | [`UC-USR-08`](use-cases/uc-usr-08-sign-out.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-009` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-14` | Request password recovery. | A valid email starts the email-based flow; the response must not reveal whether an account exists. | [`UC-USR-09`](use-cases/uc-usr-09-request-password-recovery.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-010` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-15` | Reset a password through a link. | A valid token plus valid password/confirmation completes the change; used, invalid or expired links cannot be reused. | [`UC-USR-10`](use-cases/uc-usr-10-reset-password.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTH-011` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-16` | Confirm an email change through a web link. | /confirm-email-change consumes the token and reports the outcome; the old email remains until the new one is confirmed. | [`UC-USR-11`](use-cases/uc-usr-11-confirm-email-change.md) | `RF-WEB-AUTHX-004` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-17` | View account identity and status. | Available username, name, avatar, email, roles, registration date and verification are shown. | [`UC-USR-13`](use-cases/uc-usr-13-view-profile.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-001` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-18` | View personal statistics. | The view fetches backend metrics and distinguishes loading; presentation depends on platform and role and is not a physical bottle inventory. | [`UC-USR-13`](use-cases/uc-usr-13-view-profile.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-003` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-19` | Edit the exposed personal details. | Username and first/last name are saved after validation; email is not changed as part of this edit. | [`UC-USR-14`](use-cases/uc-usr-14-edit-profile.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-002` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-20` | Upload an avatar from a file. | Profile lets the person upload an image from the browser (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP up to 5 MB); identity updates after a successful upload. There is no field to enter a URL. | [`UC-USR-15`](use-cases/uc-usr-15-change-avatar.md) | `RF-WEB-WEBX-008` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-21` | Change a local password. | With `hasPassword` the current password is verified and the new one confirmed, even when a social provider is linked; an account without its own password requests a link to set a Vinnot password. | [`UC-USR-16`](use-cases/uc-usr-16-change-password.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-004` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-22` | Request an email change. | The new email must be valid/available and different; confirmation is sent and the old email remains active until completion. | [`UC-USR-17`](use-cases/uc-usr-17-request-email-change.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-005` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-23` | Deactivate an account with confirmation. | The account becomes inactive and sessions are revoked; this action is not described as permanent deletion. | [`UC-USR-18`](use-cases/uc-usr-18-deactivate-account.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-006` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-24` | Permanently delete with typed confirmation. | Requires «acepto borrar cuenta» in ES or «I agree to delete my account» in EN; only outer whitespace is ignored and submission requires a match. | [`UC-USR-19`](use-cases/uc-usr-19-delete-account.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-007` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-25` | Cancel or recover a failed deletion. | Closing resets the phrase; failure retains the account and reports an error, while success signs out and removes local data. | [`UC-USR-19`](use-cases/uc-usr-19-delete-account.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-008` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-26` | Respect an Apple-linked identity. | An Apple link requires fresh authorization before revocation/deletion; missing credentials or failed revocation prevent deletion completing. | [`UC-USR-19`](use-cases/uc-usr-19-delete-account.md) | `RF-WEB-ACCOUNT-009` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-27` | Select Spanish or English and retain the preference. | Selector updates interface/formats and AI requests; free-text catalogue/user content is not automatically translated. | [`UC-USR-20`](use-cases/uc-usr-20-change-language.md) | `RF-WEB-UX-001` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-28` | Present Vinnot and discovery entry points. | Home offers types, grapes, wines, mobile scanning, events and routes; opening protected features leads to login. | [`UC-USR-22`](use-cases/uc-usr-22-home.md) | `RF-WEB-HOME-001` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-29` | Navigate through header, menu and footer. | Offers product routes, profile/cellar, language and public links; administrative actions depend on role. | [`UC-USR-23`](use-cases/uc-usr-23-navigate.md) | `RF-WEB-HOME-002` |
| `RF-WEB-USR-30` | Reset scroll on page changes. | Navigating from the footer to legal/contact starts at the top of the new page; paginated pages also return to the top. | [`UC-USR-23`](use-cases/uc-usr-23-navigate.md) | `RF-WEB-HOME-003` |
