# Presentation

The oral presentation of Vinnot as animated slides: 25 slides that present the project
from scratch, for people who do not know the platform. It goes from
what Vinnot is and what you can do, with the live demo, to how it was built, how it makes
money and what growth costs. Each slide shows which rubric rows it covers and links to the
page of this documentation that explains the topic.

<Link className="button button--primary button--lg" to="/presentation">Open the presentation</Link>

## How to use it

The presentation fills the window. To project it, press **F** or the full-screen
button.

| Action | Keys or gesture |
|---|---|
| Forward | → , ↓ , space, Enter, Page Down or a click on the slide |
| Back | ← , ↑ , Backspace or Page Up |
| Go to the start or the end | Home or End |
| Full screen | F |
| On a phone or tablet | Swipe left or right |

Each slide reveals its content in steps: moving forward shows the next step before going
to the next slide. The address keeps the slide number (`/presentation#12`), so reloading
the page resumes at the same place. At the bottom there is a clock with the elapsed time
and a progress bar split by chapter. Transitions are not animated when the system asks
for reduced motion.

## Figures it shows

The figures on the slides come from the pages linked in the table above and are checked
by the site tests (`docs-site/test/presentation-content.test.mjs`): the requirement
totals, the monthly cost, the projection by users, the sample commission and the seal
thresholds. The user projection and the winery prices come from
`docs-site/src/components/Presentation/growth.mjs`, and
`docs-site/test/presentation-growth.test.mjs` checks them against their pages. When one
of those pages changes a figure, update `content.mjs` or `growth.mjs` in
`docs-site/src/components/Presentation/`.
