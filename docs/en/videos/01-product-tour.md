## Script

### 1. What Vinnot is

- Open the app signed out and see the welcome screen.

### 2. Create the account

- Sign up with email and accept the terms.
- Open the verification link and sign in.
- Recover the password from "Forgot your password?" and choose a new one from the link.

### 3. First steps

- Switch the language between Spanish and English.
- Go through the tab bar.

## Outside the app

These steps happen outside the app, so the video does not show them:

- The verification and recovery links arrive by email; the video opens them straight in the app.
- "Continue with Google" and "Continue with Apple" appear on sign-up; signing in continues on the Google or Apple page, and back in Vinnot you choose the username.

## Preparation

- A test inbox to verify the account.
