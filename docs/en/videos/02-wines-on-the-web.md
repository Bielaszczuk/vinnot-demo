## Script

### 1. Find a wine

- Search by name, filter by type, country and region, and go through the results.
- Open the wine page.

### 2. Learn more about it

- Open the AI sommelier profile.
- Compare two wines.

### 3. Review and save

- Publish a review, edit and delete it; like and comment on another.
- Add the wine to My Cellar with a note and rating.
- Share the wine.

## Preparation

- A wine with an AI profile in both languages.
