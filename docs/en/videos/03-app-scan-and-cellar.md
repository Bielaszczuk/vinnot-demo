## Script

### 1. Set up the app

- Install from the store, sign in and allow notifications.

### 2. Scan

- Scan a known label and open the wine; the photo is saved when the wine had no image.
- Scan an unknown label, search by hand and propose the wine.
- Follow its status in My submitted wines.

### 3. Your cellar

- Add the wine to My Cellar, edit the note and remove another one.
- Publish the note as a review and look at the statistics.

### 4. Notifications

- Receive a push notification and open it.

## Still to record

- Scanning and the push notification are recorded on a real phone: the simulator has no camera and gets no push.

## Preparation

- A real phone for the camera, one known label and one that is not in the catalog.
