## Script

### 1. Discover

- See events as a list and on the map, and filter by area.
- Open an event and see the place, date and spots.

### 2. Register

- Register for a free event and confirm attendance.
- Ask for a spot in an event that needs the organizer's approval.

### 3. After registering

- Open My tickets and one ticket.
- Cancel a registration.

## Outside the app

These steps happen outside the app, so the video does not show them:

- Paying for a paid event is completed on Mercado Pago: the registration waits for the payment and, when you come back, Vinnot shows the result.
- When the event has an organizer contact, the event page shows it and registering opens email or WhatsApp to write to them.

## Preparation

- Two published events: a free one with attendance confirmation and one that needs the organizer's approval.
