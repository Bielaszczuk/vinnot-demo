## Script

### 1. Profile

- Edit the profile, phone number and photo.
- Change the email confirming your identity and sign in again with the new one.
- Choose email and app notifications, and change the password.

### 2. Community

- Report a review and block a person.
- Follow reports and blocks in Community and safety.

### 3. Help and closing

- Open help, legal documents and the contact form.
- Show where to deactivate or delete the account and sign out.

## Outside the app

These steps happen outside the app, so the video does not show them:

- The email change is confirmed from the link sent to the new address.
- Every news email includes a link to unsubscribe.
- Without a connection, the app shows a screen to try again.
- The contact form sends the request to the support team.

## Preparation

- A second account to report and block.
