## Script

### 1. Get ready

- Open My events.

### 2. Publish

- Create a free event with approval and publish it.
- Choose online payment with Mercado Pago and see the prompt to link the account before publishing a paid event.
- Edit a published event.

### 3. Manage

- Approve, wait-list and reject registrations.
- Cancel an event with a reason.

## Outside the app

These steps happen outside the app, so the video does not show them:

- Linking Mercado Pago is authorized on Mercado Pago and, back in Vinnot, the account shows as linked in Event payments.
- When a paid event is cancelled, online payments are refunded automatically through Mercado Pago.
- The organizer gets email and app notices when new registrations arrive.

## Preparation

- An account with the partner role.
- A second account to register.
