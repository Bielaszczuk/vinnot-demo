## Script

### 1. Catalog

- Create and edit a wine, a winery and a route with stops.
- Manage events and cancel one.

### 2. People

- Change an account role, confirming your identity.
- Suspend and reactivate an account.

### 3. Communication

- Prepare an announcement, preview it, send yourself a test and send it.
- Turn a push type on and off and see its results.

## Preparation

- An administrator account and a test account to change its role and suspend it.
