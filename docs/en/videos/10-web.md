## Script

### 1. Home and sign-in

- Walk through the home page as a visitor.
- Sign in with email and password.

### 2. Wines

- Browse the catalog of Argentine wines and search for a wine by name.
- Open the wine detail and its sommelier profile.
- Go to the winery page.

### 3. Events and routes

- See nearby events and open one.
- See the wine routes and open one.

### 4. Your cellar and administration

- Walk through My Cellar.
- Open the administration dashboard.

## Preparation

- Record at desktop size (1440 × 900) and export at 1280 wide.
- A development-environment administrator account, with the location set to Mendoza so nearby events appear.
